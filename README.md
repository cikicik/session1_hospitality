# 🎭 Situasi Tamu Generator

*Welcome to the drama club — where every tap serves up a fresh scenario.*

This ain't your average list of guest encounters. Oh no. This little web app spins the wheel of *awkward*, *funny*, and *huh?!* situations so you can practice hospitality English like a pro with a splash of chaos. Bilingual? Yup. Fabulous? Absolutely.

## 💡 What It Does

Click the magic button and BOOM 💥 — one **random guest situation** appears in:

- 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia (for the vibes)
- 🇬🇧 English (for the upgrade)

Use it for:
- 🔸 Hospitality staff roleplay
- 🔸 English teaching with attitude
- 🔸 Ice breakers that aren’t cringe (maybe)
- 🔸 Your daily dose of unpredictability

## ✨ Features

- 🎲 Random situation each time
- 🧠 Bilingual content (ID + EN)
- 🎯 Straight to the point
- 🎨 Simple, clean, and cute UI
- 📱 Mobile-friendly — obviously
